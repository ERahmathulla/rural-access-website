# Editing the Rural ACCESS website

This guide is for Rural ACCESS officers. You don't need any technical experience, and you can't break the website design by editing content here.

## Logging in

1. Go to the website address and add `/studio` to the end. The page isn't linked anywhere on the site, so bookmark it.
2. Sign in with the method you were invited with (Google, GitHub, or email and password).

If you can't sign in, ask the President to invite you. See [Inviting new editors](#inviting-new-editors).

You'll see a menu with five items: Site settings, Upcoming events, Past events, E-board, and Volunteer opportunities. You can ignore the Releases tab at the top.

## What to know first

- **Nothing changes until you press Publish.** Your edits save as a draft automatically. The Publish button is at the bottom right.
- **Changes take about two minutes to appear.** If you don't see yours, wait a minute and refresh the page again.
- **All times are Eastern**, whatever time zone your computer is in.
- **Every field has a short note under its name** explaining what it's for.
- **Hidden keeps something off the website without deleting it.** Turn it on for anything you want to pause or bring back later.

## Adding an event

1. Click **Upcoming events**, then the **+** at the top of the list.
2. Fill in the **Title** and choose a **Type**. Visitors can filter the events list by type.
3. Set the **Start**. Add the **End** if you know it. If you leave End empty, the event stays listed for 2 hours after it starts.
4. Add a **Location name** and **Address**. An address adds a Map link to the event.
5. Add a **Description** if it helps.
6. Choose the **Audience**:
   - **Open to everyone** gets a gold tag that stands out, so non-FSU visitors can find it.
   - **FSU students**
   - **Members only**
7. Choose the **Points**. "No points" shows no points tag.
   - **No points**
   - **1 point**
   - **1 point (+½ for setup or cleanup)**
8. For independent volunteering, put the host in **Organization**. The event will show "Hosted by" and that name.
9. If people need to sign up, paste the link in **Sign-up link**. You can also set a **Sign-up deadline**. Once it passes, the Sign up button is replaced with "Sign-up closed".
10. Press **Publish**.

Every event gets an "Add to calendar" link automatically.

**After an event ends** it leaves the website and moves to Past events on its own. There's nothing to clean up.

**To cancel an event**, open it, turn on **Hidden**, and press Publish.

## Updating the e-board each year

The Meet the E-Board section on the Home page appears as soon as one officer is published.

**To add someone:**

1. Click **E-board**, then the **+** at the top of the list.
2. Enter their **Name** and choose their **Role**. The website sorts officers by role automatically, President first.
3. Add a **Photo** if you have one. Without a photo, the website shows their initials. If you add a photo, you must also fill in **Alt text**, a short description of the photo for people using screen readers.
4. Add a **Short bio** of up to 200 characters if you like.
5. Press **Publish**.

**To remove someone**, open them, click the **...** menu next to the Publish button, and choose **Delete**. To keep their entry for later, turn on **Hidden** and press Publish instead.

The **Order** field only matters when two people share a role. Lower numbers show first.

## Adding a volunteer opportunity

1. Click **Volunteer opportunities**, then the **+** at the top of the list.
2. Fill in the **Title**, **Organization**, and **Summary**.
3. Choose the **Status**.
4. If it's Open, add the **Requirements** (one short item per line), **How to start**, and a **Points note** if it earns points. Email addresses you type in How to start become links.
5. Set the **Order** to choose where the card sits. Lower numbers show first.
6. Press **Publish**.

### Switching "Coming soon" to "Open"

A Coming soon card only shows its summary and a "Details coming soon" note. To open it:

1. Open the opportunity and change **Status** to **Open**.
2. Add the **Requirements** and **How to start**. Update the **Summary** if it still says details are coming.
3. Press **Publish**.

## Editing site settings

Click **Site settings**. There is only one of these, and it can't be deleted.

- **Join link (FSU HQ):** paste our FSU HQ page address here and press Publish. Every Join button on the site will go there. While this is empty, Join buttons open an email to ruralaccessatfsu@gmail.com instead.
- **Membership requirements:** one short item per line. These appear on both pages.
- **Points summary:** the text under Points & active status.
- **Health screening selection:** the text under Health screenings.

Points summary and Health screening selection work like a simple word processor, with bold, italics, bullets, and links.

The About text and mission on the Home page aren't in the Studio. Changing them needs a code update.

## Inviting new editors

Only a project Administrator can do this, which should be the President.

1. Go to [sanity.io/manage](https://www.sanity.io/manage) and sign in.
2. Open the project named **Club Website**.
3. Open the **Members** tab and choose the option to invite someone.
4. Enter their email address and choose the **Editor** role.
5. Send the invite. Once they accept it, they can sign in at `/studio`.

**When officers change each year:**

- Invite the new officers as Editors.
- Make the incoming President an **Administrator**, so they can invite people next year.
- Remove anyone who has left from the Members tab.
